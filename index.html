Research

What I work on

Short version: kernels, graphs, geometry, and the annoying but useful question of what representation a model actually learns.

Graph learning

Non-negative kernel graphs, sparsification, and structure-aware representations.

Signal processing

Time-series modeling, EEG, and methods that respect the underlying signal geometry.

Representation learning

Measuring what neural embeddings store, and when geometric summaries are actually useful.

Biomedical imaging

MRI, MPI, and image quality questions with a practical engineering angle.

Geometry-aware graph construction

Interpretability

NNK graph interpretation of deep networks

We use non-negative kernel graphs to summarize penultimate-layer embeddings and study geometric properties that correlate with performance and augmentation behavior.

Computer vision

Smart ad placement on videos

We match ads to scene context using surface geometry, semantic similarity, saliency, and scene-change cues so the placement feels less like an interruption and more like an accident humans willingly pay for.

Privacy and vision

Face and license-plate anonymization
