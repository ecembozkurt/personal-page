The thread connecting the work

Across graph learning, foundation-model embeddings, and signal analysis, the recurring question is simple: what structure is actually present in the data, and how should an algorithm adapt to it?

Geometry-aware learning

Local graph and kernel methods that adapt to heterogeneous density and manifold structure.

Spectral structure

Using effective rank and related spectral quantities to diagnose representation quality and graph behavior.

Foundation-model embeddings

Studying when learned embeddings preserve useful geometry for retrieval, fusion, and downstream inference.

Signal & image processing

Methods grounded in physical and statistical structure, from medical imaging to time-series signals.

Featured research story

Adaptive spectral bandwidth control

View full project →

IEEE MLSP 2026 · with Antonio Ortega

Finding the scale where the kernel sees the right geometry.

This work proposes a per-node bandwidth criterion that matches the effective rank of a Gaussian-kernel operator to local intrinsic dimension, while anchoring the search in a manifold-consistent log-log regime. On CIFAR-100 SSL embeddings from six encoders, the adaptive approach improves graph-based inference over fixed and competing adaptive bandwidth choices.
